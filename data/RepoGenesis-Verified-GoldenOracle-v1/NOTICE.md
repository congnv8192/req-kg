## Expert-supervised repositories (24)

The 16 Python and 8 Java repositories under `expert_supervised/` were produced for

generated by Gemini 3 Pro under expert supervision. They are original to this project and
carry no third-party licence. They are shared under `TERMS_OF_USE.md`.

These are third-party open-source projects. RepoGenesis used them unmodified at a fixed
commit; the copyright stays with their respective authors and their own licences apply.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `rock-paper-scissors-flask` | <https://github.com/prof-rossetti/rock-paper-scissors-flask> | CC BY-NC 4.0 | pinned commit |

Exact commits are in `real_world/pins.json`. Because these five carry copyleft or
non-commercial terms, this archive pins them rather than redistributing their source. Obtain
them from upstream with `real_world/fetch_oracles.sh` and comply with each project's licence.
Two deserve explicit attention:

- **synapse** is AGPL-3.0-only. Distributing modified versions, including over a network,
  triggers the AGPL source-provision obligations.
- **rock-paper-scissors-flask** is CC BY-NC 4.0, which prohibits commercial use and is not an
  OSI-approved open-source licence.

`django-rest-framework-crud` is the exception that ships as source. Its `LICENSE` is a bare
MIT text with no copyright holder line and we could not recover an upstream URL or commit for
it; it is also the one oracle already present in the public benchmark release.
